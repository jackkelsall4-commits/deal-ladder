Route from audit into IB or PE

  1. Move into transaction services (financial due diligence) or lead-advisory corporate finance at a Big 4 or mid-tier firm, at Assistant Manager or Senior Associate. This is the standard first step out of audit, and an internal move to KPMG Deal Advisory skips the external screen.
  2. Build modelling skills alongside (a three-statement and LBO modelling course). It's the main gap between audit and deals interviews.
  3. After 1–2 years on live deals: lateral moves into IB associate roles, PE portfolio and value-creation teams, or PE investment associate roles.
  4. Apply now to roles marked open to audit backgrounds or deal experience a plus; treat the hidden "requires deal experience" roles as next-step targets.
